Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your email. You'll receive a reset link within minutes.

How do I add a new patient?

Navigate to the Patients section from the dashboard and click "Add New Patient" to fill in the patient details.

Can I export patient reports?

Yes! Go to Reports & Analysis, select your filters, and click the Export button to download as PDF or CSV.

Who can access patient records?

Only authorized users with the appropriate role (Admin, Doctor, Nurse) can access patient records based on permissions.
